Product thinking

Three outside-in hypotheses I'd love to test.

I only see public information, so treat these as sharp questions rather than answers. Real data would confirm or kill them within weeks.

1 · Make the free first call a habit

Hypothesis

Users who get a clear, specific takeaway in the first call come back more often than users who don't.

Test

Instrument first-call outcome (rating, re-call within 7 days). Trial a short pre-call intake so minutes go to the answer.

Metric

Day-7 repeat consult rate.

2 · Localise trust for new markets

Hypothesis

International users need different trust signals and payment framing than Indian users.

Test

Run region-wise funnel comparisons and test proof points: verification steps, refund guarantees, session previews.

Metric

Wallet-recharge conversion by country.

3 · Help users choose the right astrologer

Hypothesis

With thousands of experts, choice overload lowers satisfaction. Better matching raises it.

Test

A/B a guided "pick for my question" flow against the browse list, with topic, language, price and rating.

Metric

Time to first call, session rating.

Rooted in public facts: first call free, 13+ languages, tens of thousands of astrologers, rising international share. I haven't seen internal data and would validate everything with your team first.
